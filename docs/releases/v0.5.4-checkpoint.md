# Dyna Brothers v0.5.4 Beta

- Dyna Brothers 2 Special uses the visible camera position for sprites during the first Sega Channel stage's opening pan and while holding B and moving the D-pad.
- The complete Sega Channel Special opening and the first island showdown are now in English. The Japanese setting still uses the original dialogue.
- Borderless gameplay comes to the foreground on launch.
- DB2's widescreen menu renderer does less work drawing the margins around selectors.

Bring your own compatible game dumps. The Windows x64 ZIP includes the launcher and both games.
